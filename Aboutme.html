About me

How it started

At first it was a casual hobby, just spending weekends working on my own custom car, getting everything exactly how I wanted it. But then it evolved — once the car became motor ready, I wanted more. To keep track of where I get things, as well as to see how far I've come in my journey, I made my very own site that shows anyone who is interested my own custom car. Then I took it a step further, as people on the street kept asking how I did it and how they could be like me, so I decided to also make it a small hub that can help grease monkeys like myself find their own car, as well as see where to get the parts and other recommendations based on what they are going for.
